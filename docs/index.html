Project 01

Mechanical design · Fusion · 3D printing

The triangulated
45° pivot

Model an integrated octagonal frame, print its parts, and assemble a freely moving joint around a metal shaft and two bearings. Then measure how it behaves under load.

Start with small fit samples and work through the design decisions one step at a time. The guide explains both what to do and why.

Prototype status The guide includes digital geometry and slicing checks. This design has not yet been physically printed, assembled, or load-tested; the measurement lesson is part of finding out how it performs.

From a sketch to a measured result

  1. 01

    Model

    Use parameters, constraints, and editable checkpoints to understand how the geometry fits together.

  2. 02

    Print

    Check fit samples, choose orientation, and inspect the sliced layers before committing to the housings.

  3. 03

    Assemble

    Work through the bearing stack, shaft, fasteners, and tool access with a clear order of operations.

  4. 04

    Measure

    Record baseline, loaded, and recovered readings, then use the evidence to guide one revision.
